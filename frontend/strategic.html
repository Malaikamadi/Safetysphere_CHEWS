  • CHW Surveillance Logs
AI Models
  • XGBoost Spatial — Flood Risk
  • LSTM Ensemble — Malaria
  • Random Forest — Heat Stress
  • Composite Vulnerability Scorer
Methodology
  • 7-day rolling prediction window
  • District-level aggregation
  • WHO threshold calibration
  • Monthly model retraining
